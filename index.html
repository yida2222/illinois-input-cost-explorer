Illinois Input Cost Explorer

A transparent look at reported farm input prices and how hypothetical cost changes alter published 2027 crop-budget returns. For discussion and education; not a farm recommendation or forecast.

Scope: Central Illinois, high-productivity farmland. The price reports below are distributor asking-price averages, not a farm's realized purchase prices. Budget sensitivities are user-entered assumptions. No individual farm data are used. Observed quotes and the 2027 budget refer to different dates; the page does not estimate when or how quoted prices reach a farm budget.
