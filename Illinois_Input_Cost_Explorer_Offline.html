Illinois Input Cost Explorer

A transparent look at reported farm input prices and how hypothetical cost changes alter published 2027 crop-budget returns. For discussion and education; not a farm recommendation or forecast.

Scope: Four published 2027 Illinois budget tables: Northern, Central high-productivity, Central low-productivity, and Southern. Each compares corn after soybeans with soybeans after corn. USDA input quotes are statewide distributor asking-price averages, not region-specific farm purchase prices. Budget sensitivities are user-entered assumptions; the page does not estimate price pass-through.

Sources and interpretation

  • Schnitkey and Paulson, 2027 Crop Budgets, Tables 1–4. The source PDF was originally released in August 2026. The two selected rotation columns are used in each region; Northern and Southern tables have no productivity subclass.
